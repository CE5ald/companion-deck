Looking for your PC

Plug in the USB cable, or make sure the Companion Deck host is running.

One-time setup

Two settings on this tablet, once. After that the cable is all you need.

  1. Turn on Developer options Open About tablet and tap Build number seven times.
  2. Turn on USB debugging In Developer options. This is how the cable carries the link.
  3. Allow this computer Plug the cable in and tap Allow, ticking Always allow.

Would rather not use Developer options? Turn on USB tethering instead and the app will find the PC over the cable that way.
